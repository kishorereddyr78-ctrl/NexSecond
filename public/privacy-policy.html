Privacy Policy

A straightforward explanation of how we use personal information to support NexSecond shopping, delivery and customer conversations.

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Applies to NexSecond's website, WhatsApp support and future app services when available.

NexSecond (also styled neXsecond) provides local shopping and delivery services in the Bengaluru region of Karnataka, India. This policy describes the types of personal information we may process, why we use it, and how to contact us about privacy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use and what you choose to share, this can include:

  • Account and contact information: your name, email address, mobile number and account/login verification details.
  • Orders and delivery: products ordered, order history, delivery address, location details that you provide or permit, and details necessary to process and fulfil orders.
  • Customer support: the content of WhatsApp messages or other enquiries, your contact details and relevant order information.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, session, diagnostic and security information generated when using our website or services, where applicable.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs, bank details or other unnecessarily sensitive information in WhatsApp support chats.

2. Why we use information

  • To create and maintain accounts, send verification codes and help you sign in.
  • To display products, accept orders, check serviceability and arrange deliveries.
  • To contact you about orders, support requests, service issues and relevant transactional updates.
  • To operate, secure, diagnose and improve the website and related services.
  • To respond to legitimate privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp and automated assistance

If you contact NexSecond through WhatsApp, your messages and associated contact details may be processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform to provide support. Some responses may be automated, and we may forward support requests to our team through email.

AI testing: NexSecond is currently testing an AI-assisted WhatsApp bot with a restricted test number. During that test, fictional or test messages may be sent to Google's Gemini API. We do not currently intend to enable Gemini processing for all customer messages without separately reviewing privacy arrangements, updating our disclosures as needed and implementing appropriate safeguards. AI-generated information can be inaccurate.

4. Service providers

We use third-party technology providers where relevant to our service, including Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database and related services), Resend (transactional and support emails), Meta (WhatsApp Business) and Google (where authentication or AI testing is enabled). These providers may process data needed to deliver their respective services. Their systems and processing locations may differ from ours.

5. Cookies, location and site functionality

Our website may use browser storage, cookies or similar technology needed for sessions, preferences, security or core functionality. Location information may be used to check whether an address is within our delivery area when you provide an address or permit location access. You can manage browser permissions and controls on your device, although restricting essential functionality may affect the service.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We may share information with service providers and people involved in fulfilling your order, such as delivery personnel who need delivery details. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect the service against misuse. We do not need your passwords or OTPs to provide ordinary support.

7. Security and retention

We seek to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain personal information as necessary for the purposes described above, subject to applicable legal, operational and recordkeeping requirements, and review requests for deletion where appropriate.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact NexSecond to ask about personal information held in connection with our services, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a concern about how it is used. Please provide enough information to identify the account or order concerned; we may need to verify your request before acting. You can change device location permissions using your device settings.

For now, send a message beginning “Privacy request” to our official WhatsApp business contact: +91 63606 21574. Do not include passwords or OTPs. We will review requests in line with applicable requirements.

9. Children

We do not intentionally request children's personal data for purposes unrelated to providing our services. Where processing involving children requires additional consent or safeguards under applicable law, those requirements will apply.

10. Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy as our website, support processes, mobile applications or legal requirements evolve. Any revised policy will be published here with a new effective date.
